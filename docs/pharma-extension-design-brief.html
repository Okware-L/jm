2. Transition

How the main site should transition into the pharmaceutical side

The transition should feel intentional and calm: from mission-led storytelling into care delivery, pharmaceutical services, and clinical operations. Instead of a hard handoff, the site should guide users through trust, services, intake, and access.

1

Mission to service

Start with the existing healthcare infrastructure message, then introduce concrete care services: teleconsultation, medicine access, mobile clinic support, prescription handling, and patient follow-up.

2

Service to guided action

Replace generic CTAs with clear next steps: Book Consultation, Request Medication Support, Find Mobile Clinic, Apply as Provider, and Access Patient Portal.

3

Guided action to role-based experience

After initial engagement, users move into tailored spaces: patient, clinician, pharmacist, operations/admin, or outreach representative.

4

Role-based experience to operational continuity

The extension becomes the system of action: appointments, supply coordination, prescription workflows, records, field outreach, and reporting.

Transition tone

Calm, reliable, medically grounded, and service-oriented. The shift should feel like entering a trusted care environment, not switching to a different product.

Navigation behavior

Keep top-level branding consistent, but tighten the information architecture once users enter pharma. Fewer promotional distractions, more task clarity.

4. Visual Direction

Hospital-green design system

The extension should move away from blue/indigo accents and use a cleaner, more clinical green language that signals health, safety, and confidence.

Design mood

  • Bright, sterile, and reassuring rather than corporate-techy
  • Soft greens and white surfaces with light grey separators
  • More breathable spacing and fewer decorative distractions
  • Clinical clarity over marketing density

Interface behavior

  • Simple cards, clear section labels, consistent spacing
  • Status indicators for appointments, prescriptions, and requests
  • Friendly but professional iconography
  • Accessible contrast and legible typography for medical data

5. Summary

Brief explanation you can reuse inside the app extension

JM-Qafri’s current site presents the pharmaceutical side as part of a broader healthcare and infrastructure mission. It introduces users to healthcare accessibility, mobile clinic outreach, technology integration, and partnership-led delivery. The pharmaceutical extension should build on that foundation by turning the narrative into a practical care platform: patients can request care and medication support, clinicians can manage appointments and treatment actions, pharmacy teams can coordinate prescriptions and fulfillment, and administrators can oversee outreach, capacity, and service quality. Visually, the extension should move from blue-accented promotional styling into a hospital-green design system that feels clean, calm, trustworthy, and medically credible.

Immediate design recommendation

Build the pharma extension as a dedicated, role-aware experience that still feels connected to the main JM-Qafri brand.

Immediate product recommendation

Make the first release revolve around care access, medication flow, mobile clinic coordination, and dashboard clarity.
